For investors and funders

The window to establish the gold standard is open now.

A neutral evidence layer for high-risk medical devices, deployed and TRL-5 ready, with no comparable system in the market. This page sets out where MedPassport sits in the market and how the commercial model scales.

The medical device industry manages billions of dollars of high-risk capital equipment with no neutral, cross-boundary evidence standard. No deployed protocol addresses device lifecycle evidence across the four organisational boundaries where OEM cloud systems go silent. The academic literature proposes the concept. MedPassport runs it.

Market position

Where MedPassport sits in the market

01

The academic gap, closed

The academic literature frames blockchain-enabled Digital Product Passports for medical devices as a research frontier, a proposed concept, not a deployed system. MedPassport is deployed and TRL-5 ready, with operating regulatory bridges to FDA and EU device databases. No comparable deployed protocol exists for medical device lifecycle evidence across organisational boundaries.

02

The market already pays for this, just not yet independently

Buyers in the refurbished equipment market already treat missing service history as a red flag, not a minor gap. What is missing is a record that survives a change of vendor, platform, or ownership, one no single company controls.

03

Medical devices remain the outlier in a market that already prices verified history

High-value industries treat verified service history as a standard input to resale value, insurance pricing, and procurement decisions. The data exists in the medical device chain, held across OEM, ISO, and hospital systems, but no neutral mechanism makes it independently verifiable at the point of transfer. MedPassport is the open protocol built to close that gap.

Why now

The regulatory moment is real

EU MDR post-market surveillance obligations are lifetime requirements, FDA QMSR is now enforced, and documentation gaps in device records feature in liability proceedings and influence underwriting terms.

The window to establish a gold standard for the industry is open now. It will not stay open indefinitely.

Commercial model

Open protocol, diagnostic-first commercial layer

Neutral by design

MedPassport is an open protocol, neutral by design, operator-free by architecture, MIT licensed. No commercial relationship can prevent a credentialed actor from logging an event.

Diagnostic-first, scaling to per-device

The commercial model is diagnostic-first, scaling to a per-device layer across refurbishers, OEMs, and insurers. The space is clean. The timing is right. The infrastructure is built.
