# MedPassport — Sequence Diagrams v1.0

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** April 2026
**Author:** Tomer Saar, PMP

---

## Overview

These diagrams define the exact step-by-step flow for the two
highest-risk workflows in the MedPassport protocol. Both require
dual-signature and are the primary focus of Sprint 2 contract
development and testing.

---

## Diagram 1 — Ownership Transfer

This flow covers the transfer of a device from a refurbisher
to a hospital. Both parties must sign independently before
the transfer is recorded on-chain.
