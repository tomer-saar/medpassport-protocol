Pilot Program

Where MedPassport engagement begins

Before any pilot commitment, we work with your team to find the evidence gap. What is missing, where it matters most, and what a pilot would actually test. That conversation is short, focused, and uses your own data.

How we engage

What an engagement looks like

Every MedPassport engagement starts with a collaborative Evidence Gap Assessment. Together with your service, quality and regulatory teams, we identify where verified device evidence is missing across your organisation, and where closing that gap creates the most immediate value. That conversation defines the pilot scope, the device focus, and the right partners to bring into the process.

What is an Evidence Gap Assessment?

A structured exercise using your own device data. We map where your current evidence chain breaks across ownership, service, and regulatory boundaries. The output is a plain-language gap report and a proposed pilot scope - specific devices, event types, and what closing each gap would mean operationally. No commitment required to complete it.

If that is a conversation worth having, we would like to start it.

Why engage now

What you are evaluating, in plain terms

01

This is deployed, not a concept

MedPassport is TRL-5 and running on a public testnet today, with live regulatory bridges to FDA and EU device databases. The Evidence Gap Assessment uses this same deployed infrastructure on your own data, not a slide deck or a roadmap.

02

Your peers already treat this gap as real

Buyers in the refurbished equipment market already treat missing service history as a red flag, not a minor gap. What is missing is a record that survives a change of vendor, platform, or ownership, one no single company controls.

03

Engaging early shapes how the standard works for you

The protocol is open and neutral by design. Organisations that engage during the pilot phase help shape which event types, evidence packs, and integrations matter most, before those decisions are locked in for the broader market.
